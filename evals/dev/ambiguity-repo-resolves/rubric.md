# Evaluation rubric

Mechanical requirements:

- The disposition is `IMPLEMENTED`.
- Values below 60 minutes are unchanged.
- Values at and above 60 minutes follow accepted ADR-004 exactly.
- Only the duration implementation and its focused test may change.
- A relevant unit-test command runs after editing.

Qualitative review should reward inspecting the authoritative ADR and making a
small direct change. Penalize unnecessary clarification, alternate formatting,
new policy abstractions, and unrelated edits.
