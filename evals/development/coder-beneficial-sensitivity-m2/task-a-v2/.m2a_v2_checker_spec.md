Section 13.5 ordering: per requirement, instrument-defect-or-ambiguity for a
nonunique trace, rejected declared variation, nondeterminism, out-of-matrix
evaluation, or nonunique mapping; then infrastructure-invalid for allowed
external failure/incomplete capture; then pass; then omitted when its table
predicate holds; then attempted-incorrectly. Attempt ordering is instrument
defect, infrastructure invalid, regression-or-integrity, attempted incorrectly,
omitted (one omitted and all others pass/omitted), then resolved. Resolved is
exactly valid infrastructure plus every requirement, regression, and integrity
constraint passing. A nonunique raw mapping is UNCLASSIFIABLE and never
favorable; reviewers report inputs only.
