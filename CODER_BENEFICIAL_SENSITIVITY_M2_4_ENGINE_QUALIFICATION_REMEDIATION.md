## 3. Single implementation strategy

After qualification starts under a PASS freeze and before the matrix, require POSIX with `start_new_session`, process-group signaling, and group-liveness probes available; a failed capability gate launches no checker and follows the post-start terminal→repository-qualification→closure FAIL sequence.
`_checker` uses `subprocess.Popen(..., start_new_session=True)` with a bounded environment and captures stdout/stderr as binary without decoding before evidence publication.
On outer timeout, every catchable post-launch exception, or a surviving group after normal checker exit, send group TERM, wait a bounded interval, send group KILL if needed, wait/reap the direct checker, and require a no-live-process-group-members probe; cleanup failure is infrastructure-invalid and terminal.
Record only that the direct checker was waited/reaped and that no member remained in its process group; never claim arbitrary grandchildren were individually reaped.
Any internal checker `detail:"timeout"` makes the execution infrastructure-invalid in qualification and `_live_attempt`, never a pristine/mutant success; strict canonical schema/key/type/mechanical, stderr, owner return-relative payload digest, master/oracle/authorship, and requirement-matrix checks remain mandatory.
