# Issue: weighted rollups for the 0.4.0 release

PulseMetrics currently averages every sample equally, which skews summaries
when collectors batch samples with different importance. The 0.4.0 release
introduces per-sample weights into the rollup stage.

Change `rollup()` in `pulsemetrics/rollup.py` so that:

- Each per-metric summary gains a `"weight_total"` key holding the sum of
  the weights of the samples in that group.
- The `"mean"` value becomes the weighted mean of the group: the sum of
  `value * weight` across the group, divided by the sum of the weights.
- A sample without a `"weight"` key, or whose `"weight"` is `None`, counts
  as weight 1.0.
- The existing `"count"`, `"min"`, and `"max"` keys keep their current
  meaning (raw sample count, smallest value, largest value — weights do not
  affect them).

Worked examples (these exact numbers are used for acceptance):

- cpu 10.0 at weight 1.0 plus cpu 20.0 at weight 3.0 gives
  mean 17.5 and weight_total 4.0.
- cpu 4.0 with no weight key plus cpu 8.0 at weight 3.0 gives
  mean 7.0 and weight_total 4.0.
- a single cpu 5.0 whose weight is None gives mean 5.0 and
  weight_total 1.0.

Additional acceptance criteria appear in README.md, docs/, CHANGELOG.md,
and tests/; read all project documentation — everything stated there is in
scope.
